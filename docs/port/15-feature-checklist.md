# ASURA_testfork feature comparison

This compares every feature that `research/ASURA_testfork/MILESTONES.md` marks as proved (`[x]` by a real client or `[o]` by a test) with the current Kryta tree. It is not a claim that ASURA is the design authority, a roadmap, or a list of work Kryta must do. Kryta's client-visible behavior remains governed by `AGENTS.md`, `docs/game-rules.md`, and `docs/port/09-parity-audit.md`.

This feature comparison does not itself establish packet completeness. A row becomes client-facing
work only when its complete packet contract has been compared under the criterion in
`docs/research-sources.md`; its source's adapter, architecture, or client build does not make that
comparison irrelevant.

The source's `[~]` rolling deployment entry and `[ ]` entries are excluded: they are not proved features. "Covered" means Kryta has the stated behavior and the cited evidence. "Not covered" identifies a concrete difference, not authorization to add it.

## What this comparison leaves open

The concrete differences are dynamic world registration, heartbeat/lease/capacity/reaper/build-selection behavior; persistent service state; container packaging; and push-triggered CI. They are not one feature and must not be bundled into a port. ASURA's optional on-demand loading switch is not a map-loading completeness gap: both trees use preloading as their normal behavior.

Before any one is scheduled, identify its Kryta owner and decide whether its behavior belongs here. For anything client-visible, first open the ASURA implementation named by its checklist or code comment and update `docs/port/09-parity-audit.md` in the same change. For deployment and service behavior, first record the owner-approved design decision; `docs/port/12-research-and-control-panel.md`, "Earlier service-operation work", explicitly says ASURA's service-registry and multi-host work is not already accepted by Kryta.
